Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Silver Spring, PA

Sewage is a contamination emergency. Keep out of the water, stop using drains and call for professional cleanup.

A sewage backup in a Silver Spring home is an emergency, and the 24/7 line is open now. Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets, keep people and pets out of the water, and call the line for emergency sewage backup cleanup before you try to clean it yourself.

Sewage is category 3 water, which means it can carry bacteria and viruses. It cannot be mopped away safely, and porous materials that touched it are usually removed rather than saved.

Sewage backup: what to do first

The goal is to limit contact and stop more water from entering.

  1. Stop using every drain, toilet and fixture in the house.
  2. Keep children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system out of the area.
  3. Do not touch the water without waterproof gloves and boots. Do not use a household vacuum.
  4. Shut off power to the affected area only if you can do so from a dry spot. If the panel is wet, call 911 or the utility.
  5. Call the line, then call a plumber to find the cause.

Why sewage backs up

A blockage in your own lateral, a collapsed or root-filled line, or an overloaded municipal sewer can push wastewater back into a home. A clog that affects only one fixture is usually local, while several drains backing up at once points to the main line or the public sewer.

If more than one drain is affected, call your sewer authority as well as a plumber. In West Hempfield Township, the township says anything other than stormwater entering the storm sewer is an illicit discharge and takes reports at its municipal office.

How sewage cleanup is done

The process is more careful than ordinary water removal because of contamination.

Contain and protect

The area is isolated, and the crew wears protective clothing. Air movers are placed so they do not spread contamination to clean rooms.

Remove and dispose

Standing sewage is pumped out. Carpet, pad, soaked drywall and insulation that touched the water are removed and bagged.

Clean, sanitize and dry

Remaining surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial products, then dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. See structural drying for how readings are tracked.

Water categories compared

Knowing the category explains why sewage is handled differently from a pipe leak.

Health and safety

Sewage can carry pathogens, so questions about illness belong with a physician. Do not eat food that touched the water, and wash hands thoroughly after any contact. Do not run fans or the HVAC system through the contaminated area.

Sewage Backup Cleanup questions from Silver Spring homeowners

Can I clean up a small sewage backup myself?

It is not recommended. Even a small backup can contaminate carpet, pad and drywall, and cleaning without protection exposes you to pathogens.

Is a clogged toilet a sewage backup?

A single toilet overflow with clean water may be a minor loss, but any contaminated water, or backup from multiple drains, needs professional assessment.

Will insurance cover sewer backup?

Often only with a specific endorsement. Check your policy for sewer or drain backup coverage and ask your insurer.

How long before it is safe to use the room again?

Not until it has been cleaned, sanitized and dried, and readings show it is dry. A professional can confirm that.

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Silver Spring?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open at night and on weekends, and sewage calls get same-day response when a professional is available. Stay out of the water and stop using drains.
