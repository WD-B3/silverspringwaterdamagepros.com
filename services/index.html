Water Damage Restoration Services in Silver Spring, PA

Every water damage service we cover in Silver Spring and West Hempfield Township, grouped by cause. Call the 24/7 line if water is spreading now.

Water damage comes from many sources, and the right response depends on where the water started, how long it has been there and what it has touched. The pages below are grouped by cause so you can find the one that matches what you see.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the 24/7 line and describe the situation. A restoration professional will point you to the right next step. Our water extraction page is the best place to start when there is standing water.

Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

Split and pressurized supply lines flood fast, so shutoff, extraction and drying all start together.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Winter breaks usually appear after the thaw, and the cleanup starts with shutoff, extraction and drying.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Tank failures, fitting leaks and relief valve discharge that flood a utility room or basement.

Slab Leak Water Damage

Leaks in supply lines under the slab, found by detection and followed by drying and flooring repair.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washing machine, dishwasher and refrigerator failures that wet floors and cabinets.

AC Leak Water Damage

Clogged condensate lines and failed drain pans that stain ceilings and walls.

Kitchen Water Damage

Swollen cabinets, under-sink leaks and wet kitchen floors that need opening and drying.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sagging and collapse, whether the water came from the roof, a bathroom or an attic duct.

Storm Water Damage

Rain through roofs, windows and walls after wind or storm damage to the building envelope.

Flood Damage Cleanup

River, surface and groundwater flooding needs safety checks, extraction, sanitizing and careful drying.

Basement Water Damage

Sump failures, seepage and leaks in below-grade rooms and finished basements.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Wet joists, vapor barriers and insulation in the void under the floor.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

Pumps, wet vacuums and extraction wands remove standing water before it soaks into the structure.

Structural Drying

Moisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers that dry a building to a measured finish.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Category 3 contaminated water, containment, removal and sanitizing after a backup.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Cupping, crowning, buckling and staining in solid and engineered wood and laminate.

Carpet Water Damage

Wet carpet, pad and tack strip, with advice on drying in place or replacing.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Drywall, baseboards, insulation and wall cavities that stayed wet after a leak.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Offices, shops and multi-unit buildings where downtime and tenants matter as much as the water.

Secondary service

Mold Remediation

Containment, removal and moisture control after a leak has left growth behind.
